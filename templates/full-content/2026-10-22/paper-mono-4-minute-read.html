At Paper, we couldn’t find the mono we wanted – so we made it. Beautiful for headlines, text, user interfaces, and code alike.

1 Design

Timeless character

Flat terminals, gently squared curves, and a balanced x-height give Paper Mono the familiar character of a classic sans.

Variable weights

You have 8 weights to work with, and the variable font lets you fine-tune the weight anywhere in between.

Beautiful numbers and symbols

We designed numbers and symbols with utmost care, so that they’re pleasing to look at and effortless to read.

Box-drawing characters

Build tables, diagrams, and progress bars directly in text, using 128 box-drawing characters and 32 blocks.

Coding ligatures

A deliberately small set of ligatures that makes reading code enjoyable and doesn’t overwhelm you with unfamiliar shapes.

Duospace characters

Probably our favorite feature of the font, duospacing helps text flow naturally while keeping the monospace feel.

Narrow spaces

Enable a little less space between words to help paragraphs flow or to let inline code sit naturally within a sentence.

Small arrows

We’ve included an understated alternate arrow style that works alongside text without drawing too much attention.

Single-story a

You can use the single-story “a” to give the text a warmer, more informal voice.

Slashed zero

By default, the zero is unslashed to keep the numerals looking clean. You can add a slash when you need to tell 0 and O apart.
