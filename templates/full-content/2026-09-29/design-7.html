Questions, answered.

Is Pro really a one-time payment of $9?

Yes. Lumiko Pro is currently available as a $9 lifetime deal (discounted from the regular $29 price). Pay once and it is unlocked on your account forever. No subscriptions, renewals, or recurring fees.

Can Lumiko record desktop apps outside the browser (like VS Code or Figma)?

Yes! Even though Lumiko installs as a lightweight Chrome extension, it uses Chrome desktopCapture to record your entire screen, external monitors, or any standalone desktop application (VS Code, terminal, Figma, Slack, etc.) with automatic cursor zoom.

What happens to my videos if I stay on Free?

You get almost everything! Unlimited recording, auto-zoom, Smart Blur, Custom Backgrounds, and up to 1440p exports are completely free forever.

Can I remove the watermark without upgrading?

The "Made with Lumiko" watermark is part of the Free tier and helps others discover Lumiko from your videos. Upgrading to Pro removes it completely for clean, white-labeled exports.

Do you store or see my recordings?

No. Recording and export processing happens locally in your browser. We do not upload, view, or retain your screen recordings. See our Privacy page for details.

Which resolutions does each tier support?

Free exports up to 1440p (including standard 1080p HD). Pro unlocks full 4K rendering.
