OpenAI is rolling out Images 2.6 globally in ChatGPT and Codex across every subscription tier on desktop, mobile, and the web, while GPT-Image-2.6 Flare and Sunburst are now available through the API. OpenAI says the release generates images 50% faster than before, as use across its products has reached 4 billion images per week.

The update adds GIF creation, allowing users to turn characters, mascots, product shots, sketches, reactions, and storyboards into short animated loops. It also aims to keep characters, products, and brand details consistent through successive editing rounds.

For developers, Flare targets speed and professional layouts, including posters and product imagery. With low quality selected, it can produce an image in as little as six seconds. Sunburst is designed for greater detail and more consistent edits at medium or high quality.

Images 2.6 also brings ChatGPT's image markup to the API, so teams can identify precise areas for changes. SVG exports prepare designs for websites, apps, and print, while a new complexity parameter controls how much planning the model applies. OpenAI recommends low complexity for most quick edits, reserving medium or high settings for scenes with many elements or constraints.
