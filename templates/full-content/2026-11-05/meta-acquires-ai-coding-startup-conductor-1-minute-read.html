Conductor is joining Meta

Conductor is joining Meta. Thank you for coming along for the ride!

We'll be winding down Conductor over the next weeks and months:

  • Conductor Cloud goes read-only on November 13 and shuts down on November 20.
  • Local Conductor keeps getting new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027.

To move a cloud workspace and its chats to your Mac, right-click it and choose Transfer to local. To download all your data, go to Settings → Account → Export data.

If you need anything, email [email protected].

Improvements

  • Add your own completion sounds in Settings → General. Pick a sound file, name it, and choose it like any other sound.
  • Archived cloud workspaces show their PRs, last known changes, and file tree in the right panel.
  • Added GPT-6.1 Terra to the Codex model picker.

Fixes

  • Fixed the Claude account usage display when Claude Code reports newer limit data.
  • Fixed closed chats in chat history showing a recent time when nothing had happened in them.
  • Messages that agents send to other agents now show a "Send message" label with the recipient and contents.
  • The rename workspace dialog now prefills the name shown in the sidebar instead of the worktree name.
  • Long PR titles no longer hide the PR number in the PR view.
  • Expanded Mermaid diagrams fill the window instead of opening in a narrow popup.
