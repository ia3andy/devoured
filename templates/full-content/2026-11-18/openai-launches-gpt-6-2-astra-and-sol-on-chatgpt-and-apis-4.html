OpenAI has launched GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol, a new model pair built for professional work. Astra is the company's frontier model for the hardest projects, while Sol is its efficient, versatile model for everyday tasks. Both are rolling out now to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and to developers through the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol. OpenAI says both models produce better results on demanding tasks with fewer output tokens, so users get more done within the same usage limits.

Astra leads several benchmarks. On Agents' Last Exam, which tests multistep professional work, it scores 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra at less than a third of the cost and 5.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at 5% of the cost. OpenAI calls it the best model in the world for scientists, citing new highs on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, where it beats GPT-6 Astra by 9 percentage points, and the Vals Science Index. It more than doubles Fable 5.2's pass rate on Harvey LAB-AA legal tasks and ranks first on AA-3D with 1,318 Elo, ahead of Fable 5.2 at 1,271 and GPT-6.2 Sol at 1,259.

Astra builds slides, spreadsheets and documents that follow supplied templates and the conventions of law, sales, finance and accounting, matching layout, colors and typography down to font size, even from a screenshot.

It writes cleaner code, builds stronger frontends for websites, games and 3D design, and its more precise computer use reaches individual parts in CAD and simulation tools. Early testers including Harvey, Legora and ClickUp report fewer tokens and fewer review rounds, and Basis says its long-horizon accounting work now takes 50% less time.

Sol is smarter and more efficient than GPT-6.1 Sol. It comes within 0.5 points of Astra on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 at about one-fifth the cost and outscores GPT-6 Astra on LifeVal at about one-seventh the cost.

In the API, Astra costs $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 for cached input and $40 for output, 20% less than GPT-6 Astra with a 60% cut on cached input. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's $2, $0.10 and $10 rates. Ultrafast is available with both models for Pro 500 subscribers and in the API, and reaches Pro 200 for the first time, starting with Sol.

Both models meet the Critical cybersecurity threshold and the High biological and chemical threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, so they ship with tighter cyber safeguards that also restrict some legitimate security research. Astra is OpenAI's first externally released model to reach the High threshold for AI self-improvement, and the company built Control Room, a system that can halt model output on research workloads when intervention is needed.
