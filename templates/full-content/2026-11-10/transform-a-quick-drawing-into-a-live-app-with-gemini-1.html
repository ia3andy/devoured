Transform a quick drawing into a live app with Gemini.

Great app ideas often start on paper. Turn them into interactive tools with Gemini.

Here’s how.

  1. Upload a photo of your sketch, like an app mock-up, habit tracker, or game board to Gemini.
  2. Select the Canvas tool and ask Gemini: "Can you code this app?" Gemini reads your visual and writes the code.
  3. Test your tool in the preview window. Gemini can adjust colors, fonts, or features in real time.

Next time inspiration strikes, bring it to life with Gemini.
