Questions

What is Mockreel?

Mockreel is a browser-based editor that puts a screen recording or a screenshot inside a 3D device such as an iPhone or MacBook, adds a camera move, and exports the result as an MP4 video or a PNG image.

How do I turn a screen recording into a device mockup video?

Open the Mockreel editor, drop your recording onto the page, choose a device, pick a camera move such as Hero reveal or Orbit, then open the Deliver page and render the video. It takes a couple of minutes and needs no account.

Is my screen recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Recordings, images, music and 3D models are processed in your browser and never leave your computer. Anonymous usage statistics are collected only if you allow them.

Which devices can I use?

iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone SE, Pixel 10, Galaxy S25, iPad Pro, iPad mini, Android tablet, MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, Windows laptop, iMac, Studio Display, Apple Watch, Browser window, Plain card. You can also load your own 3D model as a .glb file and choose which part of it is the screen.

What can I export?

MP4 video (H.264 with AAC sound) at 720p, 1080p or 4K, at 30 or 60 frames per second, in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3 shapes, plus 4K PNG stills. There are presets for YouTube, X and LinkedIn, for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, for the Instagram feed and for square posts, and all four shapes can be rendered in one go.

Can I zoom in or highlight part of the recording?

Yes. On the Effects page you can add a zoom into one area of the screen, a blur that softens everything else, a spotlight that dims everything else, and tap marks. Each is placed by clicking the spot on the screen and appears on the timeline.

Can I add a headline or caption to the video?

Yes. On the Text page you can add a title and subtitle above or below the device and style them: six typefaces, bold, italic, all capitals, alignment, letter spacing, your own colors and a soft shadow.

Is there a quick way to get a good-looking result?

Yes. The Media page has ready-made looks such as Studio, Midnight and Warm. One click sets the background, the camera move and the lighting, and everything can still be changed afterwards.

Can I add music or a voice-over?

Yes. The export keeps the recording's own sound, and you can add a music or voice-over file with its own volume. It starts with the clip and fades out at the end.

What camera moves are available?

Slow push, Orbit, Hero reveal, Rise, Float, Isometric, Full spin. You can also set your own keyframes: move the playhead, pose the device, and it glides between the poses.

Does Mockreel work on a phone?

Yes. The editor has a phone layout with the preview on top and the controls underneath, in a current Chrome or Safari. Rendering is quicker on a laptop or desktop, especially at 4K.
