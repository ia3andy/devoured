Nous Research is about to ship a full redesign of Hermes Desktop, and co-founder Teknium has teased a wider roadmap around it. In a short list posted on X, he named Desktop Redesign, Mobile App, Proactivity, and Bots Market, along with two unfinished entries: one reading "Google" followed by a blank, and a final line left entirely empty. Asked about timing, Teknium said the new desktop app is done, the team is fixing issues found internally, and users should be able to try it early next week.

Hermes Release Watch, an unofficial account tracking Hermes updates, shared what it calls in-progress designs. The app opens to a new home screen with every agent and its chats in one sidebar, and each agent gets its own page and settings. Routines live in one place, where users can create new ones and check every run, while skills, plugins, and tools share a single page. The designs also show light and dark themes, a phone layout, and a switch back to the classic look.

The Bots Market is next. Teknium confirmed that a bots market is coming to the Hermes Market, next to skills and plugins. According to Hermes Release Watch, picking a ready-made bot and hitting Add Bot turns it into a personal Hermes profile.

The account compared the idea to Grok Bot, except that it runs on the user's own Hermes. Early bots reportedly include Inbox Brief for triaging email, Research Desk for sourced answers, Meeting Wrap for next steps, Market Lookout for competitor changes, and Rewards Planner for card perks. Users could try each bot on a sample task first, and scheduled jobs would stay off until they pick a time.

Proactivity is the most concrete hint. Teknium openly admitted Hermes has had no real proactivity so far and asked users to comment on an open pull request that is step one of that work. With it, Hermes notices things like a flight, an appointment, a deadline, or a habit, and proposes a scheduled job on a "Schedule this job?" card.

The mobile app is also coming, with Hermes Release Watch pointing to December. The Google blank is still unexplained, though we know that Nous is working with Google to bring Gemini subscriptions into Hermes Agent. Together, these moves push Hermes, the open-source agent, toward the bot-first, always-on experience offered by Grok Bot, Muse, and ChatGPT Dots.
