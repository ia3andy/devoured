design system ↔ product connection

For as long as atomic design has been around, I've been articulating the connection between design systems and the products they serve. The design system informs & influences the products, and products inform and influence the design system.

Atomic Design Creates Connected Design Systems

Atomic design connects every layer of a design system to the real products it serves.

This vision is sound, but until recently it's been kinda hand-wavey. But I can now say I'm actually living this vision. I now have a truly system-powered product ecosystem: changes to Eddie flow into my 20+ products, and each round of product work feeds and strengthens the design system. Hooray, feedback loops!

Also, we introduced skills and talked about improving your design system foundations. Now we'll highlight some skills I'm developing to improve product quality by building with your design system:

  • product-inspection: Assess your product's quality using a similar 10-station process as the design system inspection. Get humbled, but get help in improving your product quality
  • vibe-killer: (I'm really proud of this name!) converts a vibe-coded page into one built entirely from your design system
  • ds-ai-rails - an emerging skill to assess your process for keeping AI on the rails of your design system
  • product-to-storybook sucks your product's screens into your design system's Storybook to make prototyping and front-of-the-front-end work easier
  • storybook-to-product facilitates the process of translating UI you've designed in Storybook into your real product environment
