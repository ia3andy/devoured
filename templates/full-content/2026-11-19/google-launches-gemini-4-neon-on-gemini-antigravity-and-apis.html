Google has released Gemini 4 Neon, a new member of the Gemini 4 family that brings frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion for software engineering, knowledge work, and science.

Introducing Gemini 4 Neon! Pushing the frontier across domains and punching above its model class in a meaningful way. To me, Neon feels like the right balance of intelligence, speed, and usability. Available now across @GoogleAIStudio, the API, and more!

It is rolling out now in the Gemini API paid tier in Google AI Studio and Android Studio, in Gemini for business through the API and model dropdown, in Google Antigravity for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, and in the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers. Eligible members of the Fairwind Program will start getting a cyber defense version over the next few days.

Neon sits between Carbon, built for the hardest long-horizon projects, and Hydrogen, built for fast everyday execution. It costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.15, and works with spreadsheets, PDFs, charts, audio and video in a 1 million-token context window.

Four thinking levels let teams trade intelligence for token spend, and at the default high level Google says it costs 32% less per task than Carbon. In Antigravity, Neon can act as an orchestrator directing parallel Hydrogen subagents, while in the Gemini app it now powers Tasks and deeper analysis.

In Google's comparison table, Neon scores 65.4% on CursorBench 4.1 against 58.1% for Claude Opus 5.5, plus 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 and 39.0% on SWE-sweep inside Antigravity.

Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Neon debuts at #2 on the Vals Index (70.9%), 0.4 points behind Claude Fable 5.2 at less than half the cost per task. It also takes #1 on our Finance and Legal Indexes, Tax Agent and Legal Research.

Arena says Neon debuts second on its new Agent Arena Index with 1,336 points from about 4,200 real-world agent sessions, ahead of Carbon, Hydrogen and Argon, at a median cost of $2.82 per task. That is 53% less than Claude Opus 5.5, which Neon outranks. In the Text Arena, Google now holds the top three spots, with Neon third at 1,518.
