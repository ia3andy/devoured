I. Invariant: Cheap Digital Goods Don’t Lower Physical Inflation

Cheap inference and open-weight models push the 5% consumer digital slice of CPI into ~4%–5% annual deflation by 2030. It barely dents the cost of living: hospital nursing ratios, electrical union raises, and housing keep the 95% physical services basket stuck near +3% a year. Headline inflation stays floored around ~2.5%, so the Fed remains pinned at 3.75% in Baseline and Assurance and stops cutting at 3.00% even in the Credit Crunch.
