You want the video, not the ordeal

Remocn is shadcn/ui for video. Describe your product to your AI agent, and it builds the demo video from ready-made pieces while you watch in the browser.

How it works: The whole setup is one session.

An agent, an empty folder and a single prompt. You describe, it builds, you watch it happen.

  1. Open an agent in an empty folder
  2. Paste one prompt
  3. A player opens in your browser

Seven stages. You watch, it ships.

The agent follows motion rules, checks every frame and fixes what looks off. It runs on the Claude Code, Codex, Copilot or Grok plan you already pay for.

  1. Analysis: Reads your site and repo
  2. Brand: Pulls colors, type and logo
  3. Script: Writes the beats and copy
  4. Motion: Picks blocks for each beat
  5. Build: Writes the Remotion project
  6. Choreography: Times every entrance
  7. Review: Watches it back, fixes issues

Why Remocn: Every other way costs you something.

A motion designer: $600, 2 weeks. And three rounds of “can we make it pop more”.

Remocn + your agent: 1 prompt, 1 afternoon. The animation is already built and timed. You react to the result.

  • No After Effects: Nothing to install, nothing to learn. If you can describe it, you can make it.
  • Not a screen recording: A cursor wandering across your app isn’t a demo. This looks shipped on purpose.
  • Yours to keep: The code copies into your repo. MIT, no runtime, no account, nothing to cancel.
  • Changes in seconds: Say what feels wrong and watch it change. No revision round, no queue.
