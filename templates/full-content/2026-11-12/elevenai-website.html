Meeting notes

ElevenAI records your meetings on your Mac, in person or on a call. When one ends, it writes a summary with action items, labels each speaker, and drafts your follow-up.

Tools and agents

Your notes and transcripts are available in the AI tools you already use, through MCP. You can also connect ElevenAI to ElevenAgents, for live assistance during your meetings and automated hand offs.

Privacy and security

We don’t store your audio, and your transcript history stays on your Mac. ElevenAI displays a notice on video when it is taking notes. ElevenLabs holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO/IEC 27001 certifications, among others.
