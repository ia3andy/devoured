BRCKS is construction project management software for UK builders and trades. Yes. BRCKS Chat is where the site team talks, and BRCKS Web is where the office runs every job, so together they do the job of construction project management software for builders: snags, site diary, variations, RFIs and photos in one place.

BRCKS Chat is a chat app for building sites, available on iPhone and Android. It handles one-to-one, group and project chats.
