Hi everyone,

As mentioned previously, we have revisited the proposal to make it more flexible and also address existing feedback. I’d like to share the new version of the proposal, which is included as a new tab in the same document. A summary of what changed and why:

Flexible Data Layouts

Per discussions on evolving Parquet data layout (e.g. the “non-contiguous pages” proposal), our goal is to have the new footer handle both existing and potential future layouts seamlessly. The updated proposal doesn’t assume that row groups are physical boundaries. It preserves the wording of “column chunks”, but these are simply defined to mean a contiguous set of pages for one column. Different columns may have different numbers of chunks in a file. The “row group” wording is preserved as well, but row group metadata is made optional. Lastly, stats are re-defined based on row ranges rather than row groups; different columns may publish stats at different granularities.

Page-based Representation

A big feedback point on the initial proposal was that the EncodedArray concept was opaque/confusing. It was added to efficiently model arrays in Thrift and provide random access, at the expense of readability. The updated proposal uses Parquet pages to represent modules, which yields a self-describing layout that evolves naturally as Parquet gains new encodings. It also allows for optional per-page compression.

Overall, the new proposal yields a footer that is comparable in size and performance to the original proposal, but is simpler and more flexible for handling future Parquet layout changes. We appreciate any feedback on the proposal.

Best, Jiayi
