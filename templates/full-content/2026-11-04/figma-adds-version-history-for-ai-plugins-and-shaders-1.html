Figma is adding version history to generative plugins and shaders. Announced on October 27, the update is rolling out during the week.

Editing a tool creates a new version while preserving earlier ones for review and restoration. Each version receives an agent-generated description, and the history identifies the currently published version.

For designers iterating on a custom tool or visual effect, this provides a concrete recovery point when a change produces an unwanted result. The published-version indicator also separates the version in use from later experiments.

The announcement does not specify a completion date for the rollout. The cover shows Figma’s earlier shader design mockup, not the new version-history interface.
