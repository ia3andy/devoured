Figma announced two workflow updates on October 30: a Google Drive connector for the Figma agent and Make, and internal publishing for Weave tools.

Use existing files as design context

The Drive connector can bring research, data and images into a design conversation and create new Drive files. Connectors are now available on every Figma plan, including Starter, although Google Drive is not available with the Governance+ add-on.

According to Figma’s documentation, each person authenticates their connection. Tools that write to external services are disabled by default and must be enabled separately. Source changes require another connector request to refresh the context.

Share Weave tools within a team

Organization and Enterprise users can publish Weave tools internally. They appear in the Tools tab alongside Figma’s own and Community tools. This gives teams a shared place to access their custom tools.
