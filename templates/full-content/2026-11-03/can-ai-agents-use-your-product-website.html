Questions

Will Google treat this as cloaking?

No. Agents get the same content as people, as plain text. Search crawlers see your normal pages.

Does Dedica change my website?

No. Your code, hosting and CMS stay as they are.

What happens if Dedica goes down?

Visitors fall through to your normal site.

When an agent pays, where does the money go?

Straight to your payment provider. We never hold funds.

What data can Dedica see?

Only agent requests, which power your analytics. Not what people do.

How is Dedica priced?

Free, $79 a month for Pro, and custom for Enterprise. Every plan has 24/7 support from the founders. To get started, book 15 minutes with them.

What is MCP?

The protocol assistants like ChatGPT and Claude use to connect to outside tools. Dedica gives your site an MCP server.

Is the score really free?

Yes. No signup, no card.
