Google is introducing new adaptive quality rules on Google Play to make apps work seamlessly on foldables and other large screens. Starting in April 2027, apps will need to meet at least the baseline Tier 3 standard for foldables. Those that miss the bar will no longer be able to publish or update and may get less visibility in the store. Existing apps that never get an update will remain downloadable.

Google says more than 580 million large-screen devices are already in use, from tablets and Googlebook laptops to Android XR devices. If you have ever unfolded a phone only to find a letterboxed app in the middle of the display, this is the change meant to stop it.

What do the new rules actually require?

For the basic Tier 3 bar, an app must fill the available display without letterboxing or compatibility modes, something Google has been pushing developers to fix with fold-aware layouts for a while. It also has to keep your scroll position, typed text, and media playback when you rotate the device, fold or unfold it, or resize the window. Multi-window support is also part of the baseline requirement.

Tier 2 goes further with responsive multi-pane layouts and basic support for an external keyboard, mouse, trackpad, or stylus across foldables, tablets, and other large-screen devices. Tier 1 covers tabletop and book postures along with camera continuity and interactive picture-in-picture. It also looks at whether media, video, reading, and multitasking apps can run more than one instance at a time. Google says the April mandate applies to foldables first, though the same framework will later expand to tablets, Googlebooks, and Android XR.

Heads up devs: Google Play is introducing new foldable quality requirements!

For apps, they’ll need foldable screenshots & meet Tier 3 adaptive quality guidelines starting Apr 2027.

For games, foldable screenshots are required in Apr 2027 & Tier 3 quality in Aug 2027

Games get a little more time, as they will need foldable screenshots by April 2027 but won’t have to meet the Tier 3 quality bar until August 2027, according to Google’s Mishaal Rahman, who leads Android community engagement at Google.

You will also start seeing foldable screenshots

Developers can already upload foldable screenshots, and Google says they should appear in store listings over the next few weeks. The shots are meant to show the unfolded inner display and the folded outer screen on devices such as the Pixel 11 Pro Fold and Galaxy Z Fold 8. From April 2027, new apps and updates must include three foldable screenshots apart from the usual two phone shots.

Apps that reach Tier 2 or Tier 1 can already apply for badging and extra promotion on Google Play. In August 2027, new and updated apps will also have to target Android 17, which removes the opt-outs that let developers lock orientation or block resizing on large screens. Google was already making it easier to adapt apps for laptops and foldables earlier this year, so developers have had time to prepare.
