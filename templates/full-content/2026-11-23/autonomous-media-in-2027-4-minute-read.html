Autonomous Media in 2027?

I’m working on a new post trying to chart all the different changes that I think we’ll see in 2027, and then I’ll try to make some sort of sense of them. The problem is, it's just too much for my brain to handle. I feel like I'm halfway decent at predictions if there's one or two or five variables, but I feel like there's so much changing so quickly right now that it's just really hard to map. That's what I'm going to try to do in this post.

But one that I think is going to have serious impact is AI media.

Basically, AI avatars are getting so good. Right at the moment, AI voices are becoming indistinguishable from real voices, including cadence and tone and little subtleties. That's happening at the exact same time that videos themselves are getting extremely good. And I think we’re going to see these come together next year to produce something truly strange.

I’m not quite sure what that is yet. Can’t put my finger on it.

What happens to creators

I think the major thread that I'm thinking about is, for content itself on YouTube, for content creators who make money off of being themselves and having their opinions, their mannerisms, their looks, their voice, etc., all that could change drastically.

Basically I’m already seeing tons of really good youtube content that’s fully AI. Like there was obviously a human creator behind it, like to give the ideas or the script or whatever, but everything else was automated. So it’s just more content. Of course there will be tons of slop, but many services will exist to filter that shit out and find the best stuff. But the question is, what does it look like to be a creator in 2027 when good creators can put out tons and tons of super high quality content?

And then there’s the appearance-based influencers. I’ve been hearing that OnlyFans creators are taking major competition from AI models. And I’m wondering what happens when you have actually good content (X, R, or whatever rated) that has a really attractive avatar associated with it.

Or another angle is just regular people modifying themselves to look better. To remove flaws, or to enhance their looks, so they look better in YouTube videos if they do them themselves. Or to have that avatar of them perform their actual content.

To me this is the best world for content creators, at least like myself, who care more about the ideas getting out there and being able to do a whole lot more of them at scale.

Because right now—generally speaking—only people making top 1% money can output top 1% production quality.

I have tons of ideas that I could just go narrate and trim down in Vim to turn into a perfect piece of content, and then boom: it could go out as a podcast, it could go out as a video, it could go out as a set of clips. All of that can happen automatically and look really good with extremely high production, without any slop whatsoever. That would be great.

But I feel like there are a lot of other changes similar to this that would just be massive that I can’t quite pinpoint. Any thoughts welcome!

Summary

  1. AI voices and AI video are both getting really good at the same time
  2. I think they come together in 2027 and make something pretty strange
  3. Tons of good YouTube content is already fully AI, with a human behind the ideas
  4. Appearance-based creators are already up against AI models
  5. The best version for me is my own avatar performing content I fully wrote, at way bigger scale
