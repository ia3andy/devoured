Personal AI agents should work for you without taking over your digital life. As agents become increasingly capable of automating tasks, prototyping apps, and making work and personal projects easier, control and convenience should go hand in hand. Today we are announcing the 1Password for Agents platform, which helps agents work while keeping the credentials they need secure and making access something every user can see, approve, and manage.

Planning a great event can be rewarding, but organizing one while juggling the rest of your responsibilities is not. Now you can ask an agent to handle the preparations, coordinate dates, and create and send invites, while you design an unforgettable experience.

The degree of usefulness of your agent depends on its ability to log in to the accounts and accomplish all of the tasks you’ve given it. When an agent reaches a sign-in page, the agent stops and asks for the credential that would allow it to sign in on your behalf. When this happens, you face a choice. You could take control of the agent session and manually enter your username and password into the website each time, which is slow and tedious. You could paste your password into a prompt, which means allowing the agent to have access to your credential, potentially beyond the task at hand. Neither of those are acceptable.

Now, with 1Password for Agents, your agent can request to use a credential from your vault, and if you approve the request, 1Password directly auto-fills the credentials in the destination site so the agent doesn’t have access to the credentials.

1Password is the best option for keeping your agents working without letting them see your passwords. You control what agents access and when, all the way from the prompt to the party.

How can an AI agent log in without seeing your password?

Convenience shouldn’t come at the expense of your privacy or control. An agent can make your weekly grocery list into an Instacart order or turn receipts from work trips into expense reports, but it should only access the accounts those tasks require based on the logins you approve.

Browser-password managers aren’t designed to let an agent sign in on your behalf. People who use browser-saved passwords or passwords written in notes have discovered that their agents cannot use those passwords. Each time the agent reaches a login, the agent has to stop until you take over and sign in manually before your agent can continue.

With 1Password for Agents, enabling agents to sign in is simple, fast, and completely within the users’ control.

Your passwords stay organized, and your vault stays protected. You can see and approve what will be shared and disconnect the integration at any time.
