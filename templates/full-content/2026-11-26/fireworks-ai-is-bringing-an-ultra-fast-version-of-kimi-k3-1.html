Fireworks AI is bringing an ultra-fast version of Kimi K3.1 Pro, and the pricing is quite interesting. The new endpoint is listed at $5/M input and $25/M output ($0.50/M cached input). For comparison, regular Kimi K3.1 Pro costs $2/$10, while Moonshot's official Highspeed version costs $4/$20 per million tokens. So this ultra-fast version is 2.5x the regular price and 25% more than the official Highspeed version. It also keeps the 1M context window. Fireworks says the ultra-fast mode targets 3–6x faster generation, with up to 8x for agentic coding tasks.
