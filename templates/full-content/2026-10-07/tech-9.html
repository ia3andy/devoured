“AI has the potential to reshape the entire software and product development life cycle, but realizing that potential requires a foundational change to workflows, roles, and ownership.”

Stack Overflow CEO Prashanth Chandrasekar recently spoke to Computing’s Ctrl Alt Lead podcast about why this might be the case: “With AI, every individual becomes almost a manager of agents. That's a sea change from just an individual that's writing code.” It seems that agents are superpowering some developers' work, making it easier for them to set a solo course backed up by their own team of agents—instead of taking their decreasing satisfaction at work on the chin.

“AI is forcing software organizations to document the judgment they previously relied on people to supply.”
