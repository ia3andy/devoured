Frequently asked questions

What is Krea 3?

Krea 3 is Krea's new image editing model. It is built for production: product-consistent scene changes, object removal and variants at volume.

Is Krea 3 just a smaller model?

No. Krea 3 is a new model trained for editing. It is not a distilled copy of a larger model. It spends its compute on the region you change, not on the whole image.

How is Krea 3 so fast and inexpensive?

Most edits change a small part of the image. Krea 3 finds that region first and renders only what changes.

Are these prices temporary or subsidized?

No. The launch price is the list price. We expect it to go down, not up.

What is Krea 3 good at?

Best at product scene changes, object removal and photo cleanup.

Can I self-host Krea 3?

Yes, on enterprise plans. Krea 3 runs on your own GPUs in your cloud. Talk to us about license terms and hardware sizing.

How do I test it on my own images?

Send us one workload. We run it next to what you use today and share the outputs, acceptance rate and cost.

How do I get started?

Call Krea 3 through the Krea API, or try it in the Krea app today. For volume pricing, evaluate your workload with our team.
