Agentic Commerce powered by Link, built by Stripe, and Visa

Replit can now buy what your business needs, using what it already knows about your product.

Ask it to "find 50 shipping boxes that fit this product, under $75 including delivery," and Agent uses what it knows about your business to find options and prepare the merchant's checkout. Then it waits for you to approve the final total before anything is charged.

Replit Agent coordinates the purchase while a browser subagent navigates the merchant's site, prepares checkout, and returns the purchase details to Agent for your approval.

Stripe and Visa work together to power different parts of the payment flow. Link, Stripe’s consumer network with more than 300 million users globally, lets users choose a saved payment method and approve the final total while protecting their payment details. Link facilitates the transaction scoped to the approved purchase, while Visa provides trusted payment infrastructure and capabilities designed to support secure agent-initiated transactions.

Replit Agent shops on the merchant’s website using a browser provided by Browserbase, so we don’t need a separate integration with each store. Replit Agent selects the product options and fills the cart. If you need to sign in, you can take over, then let Agent continue.

The browser session is isolated, and Agent stops at the checkout for approval. It never gets open-ended permission to spend. During payment, shell access and general browsing are disabled, and approval checks are enforced in code, not left to the model.

Agentic Commerce is available for Core and Pro users, starting in the US and limited to one-time purchases.

Agentic Ads with Google

You can now use Replit Agent to run Google ads across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover and more—all without leaving Replit.

With a few prompts in your Replit chat, you can connect or create a Google Ads account to build assets and prepare a campaign. Replit analyzes your website or app content, drafts ad copy, generates images, and recommends daily budgets. You can then review, approve, and launch in minutes.

A founder selling bid-writing software might write: “Get us in front of contractors who bid on public projects. I’m willing to spend $40 a day for 30 days.” You set the campaign goal, targeting, budget, and landing page. Replit Agent guides you with recommendations to make this simple, even if you’ve never run ads before. Because Agent built your app, it can also add the conversion tracking that ads depend on. That lets you measure campaign results through sign-ups and sales, not just clicks.

Replit users can create a new Google Ads account with the help of Agent. Replit creates and manages the account, and can ask agent for the latest performance stats and ways to optimize.

Ads with Google are available for Pro users worldwide, starting today.
