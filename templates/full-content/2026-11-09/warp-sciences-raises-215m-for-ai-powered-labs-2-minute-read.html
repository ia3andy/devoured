💥 Introducing Warp Sciences, a new company applying AI and robotics to help the world develop new medicines faster. We’ve raised a $215M Series A led by Lightspeed to bring frontier AI out of the browser and into the lab, and build scientific capacity here in America. I co-founded Warp with my long-time friend DJ Patil, FAIR co-founder Manohar Paluri, and drug hunter Solomon Reisberg. The early team is full of incredible people like Brian Spears, who helped achieve Q>1 fusion at LLNL. Here's why we're building Warp Sciences:

  1. Robotic labs will let us do science at massive scale—and generate the data to train amazing AI models for science.
  2. We need to reshore critical scientific capabilities from China, so we don’t lose our pharma industry the way we did solar, drones, and manufacturing.

We are building a next-generation preclinical CRO, in America, to make and test molecules for companies developing new medicines. We’re not developing drugs of our own. We’re building the infrastructure for the people who do. We’re starting with real chemists doing real work for real customers. We’ll progressively automate that work, with our scientists helping build the tools and proving them in the lab, one workflow at a time. More automation lets us run more experiments and generate the data to train better science models. Better models help us design better experiments and automate more of the work. The lab improves the AI, and the AI improves the lab.
