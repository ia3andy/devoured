ExcaliClaw: the collaborative workspace for hand-drawn diagrams

ExcaliClaw is an independent workspace for organizing, sharing and collaboratively creating hand-drawn diagrams. It runs in the browser, nothing to install.

Features

  • Persistent storage: every drawing is saved automatically.
  • Collections: organize your drawings into folders and search across all of them instantly.
  • Real-time collaboration: share a link, draw together and see live cursors.
  • Share by public link or by username.
  • Export everything to JSON and import drawings from other tools, such as Miro.
  • MCP tools for AI agents (Claude, Cursor, VS Code and more).

Pricing

Free €0, Starter €9/month (up to 5 members), Pro €29/month (up to 20 members), Enterprise on request. Unlimited MCP and AI on every plan.
